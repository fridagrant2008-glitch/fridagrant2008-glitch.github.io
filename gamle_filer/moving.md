      <h3>Hairbands</h3>
      <p>Hairbands was a project that grew out of a need for something to tie our hair up with and the eternal issue that everyone who needs a hairband faces: the hairbands are constantly being lost. We started to hypothesize that if everyone was always losing their hairbands, then there must be plenty of hairbands to be found.</p>
      <p>As our luck would have it, the second we started keeping our eyes out for hairbands, they started showing up everywhere. We collected on average four or five a day just from walking around the city and going through our daily motions.</p>
      <p>This led us to some pretty interesting contemplations around the idea that you find what you’re looking for, even when you might not have seen it in plain sight before you were looking. It also made us wonder what other ways we could promote sustainability and cyclical habits of use for more things in our everyday life that we typically write off as disposable.</p>
      <p>The project is a collection of the scans we took of the hairbands we found, which aim to provoke thought and conversation about what else we might just be overlooking when it comes to simple forms of sustainable relationships.</p>
